Kitten Pop: Cat Match Puzzle — Delete your data

Developer: Uni Studio. This page explains how to delete the data linked to the game Kitten Pop: Cat Match Puzzle ("the game"). The game has no accounts and we run no servers of our own, so most of it you can delete yourself in a minute. For anything else, email us.

1. Game progress on your phone

Your levels, coins, cats, boosters and settings are stored on your device. To delete them, either uninstall the game, or open Android Settings → Apps → Kitten Pop → Storage → Clear data.

2. Cloud save (only if you signed in to Google Play Games)

If you were signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is kept in your own Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. Delete it after step 1, before you open the game again (otherwise the game uploads it again):

  • Open the Google Play Games app → your profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data → under Delete individual game data, choose Kitten Pop → Delete; or
  • Open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Kitten Pop → Options → Delete hidden app data.

3. Advertising data

Our ad partners (Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, Google AdMob) link ad data to your device's advertising ID. Delete or reset it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path differs by phone maker). New ad data is then no longer linked to the old ID.

4. Ask us to delete the rest

Email game.studio.m@gmail.com with the subject Kitten Pop data deletion. We will:

  • ask GameAnalytics, our analytics provider, to delete the gameplay statistics (sessions, levels, coin use, purchases) recorded for your device; and
  • forward your request to our ad partners.

The game has no accounts, so please tell us your phone model and roughly when you last played to help match your data. We reply within 30 days.

What is kept

Google Play keeps the record of any in-app purchase you made, under Google's privacy policy; we cannot delete it. Ad partners may keep limited data for fraud prevention and legal reasons as their own policies describe. We keep your deletion email only as long as needed to handle it.

See also the Kitten Pop privacy policy.
