Kitten Pop: Cat Match Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Kitten Pop: Cat Match Puzzle ("the game"), published by Uni Studio ("we"), collects and how it is used.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, coins, cats, settings and purchases are saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation, Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These partners may collect your device's advertising ID, device model and operating system, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. You can change your choice in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings.

Partner privacy policies: Unity / LevelPlay, Google.

Analytics

The game uses GameAnalytics to understand how players progress (for example sessions, levels started and finished, in-game coin use and purchases). It uses a random device identifier and device information, not your name or contact details. See the GameAnalytics privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google shares only a purchase receipt so the game can deliver what you bought.

Notifications

The game may show local reminders (for example when a daily gift is ready). They are scheduled on your device, contain no personal data, and can be turned off in your Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data ourselves, requests about data collected by our ad and analytics partners can be made to them directly using the links above, or sent to us and we will forward them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
